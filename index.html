§4 · Electric vehicle components · Velocity Volt

8years/100kmiles

The longest coverage in the guide applies to the drive unit, the on-board charger and the high-voltage battery.

§4.2 · High-voltage battery

Two guarantees in one battery.

① Defects

Any failure from materials or workmanship.

② Capacity loss

If usable capacity falls below 70% of the original rating.

Both are limited to 8 years or 100,000 miles, whichever comes first.

70% threshold

71–100% Normal ageing

< 70% Warranty claim

§4.3 · Transferability

The warranty goes with the car.

  • Automatic. No paperwork for the next owner.
  • Free. No transfer fee.
  • Remainder of term. The clock keeps running; it doesn't reset.
Easy to miss: it sits in the EV section, but it applies to all Velocity warranties.
